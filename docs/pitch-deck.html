What LedgerFlow is

Three things. Nothing else.

Not an ERP, not full accounting. Just the order-to-cash loop, done properly — capture what happened, bill the right party, collect and reconcile.

01 · Service completion

Recorded, not assumed

02 · Invoice

Billed to whoever's responsible

LedgerFlow8

How it's stored

Nothing important is a number someone typed in.

Balances are never stored

"Outstanding" is a database view recomputed from invoices, receipts and credit notes on every read — there's no cached number that can go stale.

Issued invoices are frozen

The exact data behind an invoice is snapshotted the moment it's issued. A later change to a customer's address can't rewrite what an already-sent document says.

Payments are receipts, not invoice edits

One bank transfer can settle five invoices, or none yet. The allocation is its own record, not a guess baked into the invoice.

Every company's data is walled off in the database

Enforced by row-level security at the data layer — a bug in a screen can't leak one company's invoices into another's.

Every bill-to override, every issued invoice, every correction is written to an append-only log that nothing — not even an admin — can edit after the fact.
